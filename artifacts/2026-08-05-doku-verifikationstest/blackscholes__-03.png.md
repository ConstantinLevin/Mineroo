Fischer Black, 1975 (left) and Myron Scholes, undated (right).

This session continues to cover options by looking at the two key models for their valuation: the binomial and Black–Scholes models. It also covers the key risk measures of gamma and delta, which are the two most important of the five factors that can impact the price of an option contract, and which together are known as the 'Greeks', named after the Greek letters which represent the various quantities measuring sensitivities of option prices.

You will need to spend approximately 12 hours on this session. This can be broken into:

• 4 hours 30 minutes of reading from the module textbook and the VLE  
- 5 hours 30 minutes to work through the activities in this session  
• 1 hour of self-directed study  
• 1 hour to attend a scheduled online tutorial.
